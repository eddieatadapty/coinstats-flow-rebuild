Day 1

Pass the Apple Ads keyword into Adapty

After a one-time SDK setup, the keyword each install came from arrives as a user attribute and picks the flow at first open. No app release.

Week 1

One flow per search

Your name, “cmc” and the tracker searches each get their own first screens and paywall; every other keyword keeps today’s onboarding.

Week 2

Judge each search on what it earns

Each flow against today’s, measured on trials, paid subscriptions and what each install from that keyword earns, so bids follow the search that pays.

We found no active Meta ads from CoinStats in the Meta Ad Library; your last one ran in October 2020. On Apple Ads your share of voice is 8% on “coinstats” and 7% on “coinstats: crypto portfolio” in the US, so other apps’ ads take most of the ad slots on your own name: a brand defence worth checking. “cmc” has the highest popularity of your four terms but your share there is 1%. The search data is account-level and US only. This layer carries no range: only a test per search can give one.
